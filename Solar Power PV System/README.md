# Solar Power Photovoltaic (PV) System

**Team Project**

**Status:** Ongoing

---

## Overview

A hardware-based academic project exploring the fundamentals and practical aspects of solar photovoltaic power generation. The project covers the complete energy flow from PV modules to usable AC power, including charge controller regulation, battery storage, and inverter operation.

## System Components

- **PV Panels** — Convert solar radiation into DC electrical energy
- **Charge Controller** — Regulates voltage and current from the PV panel to prevent battery overcharge or discharge
- **Battery Energy Storage** — Stores generated power for use when sunlight is unavailable
- **Inverter** — Converts stored DC power into usable AC power for practical loads

## What Is Being Studied

- Conversion efficiency of solar energy into electrical energy
- Factors affecting PV output, including irradiance, temperature, and load conditions
- Role of the charge controller in maintaining stable power delivery
- Inverter operation for DC-to-AC conversion
- System performance under varying operating conditions
