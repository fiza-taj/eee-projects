# ☀️ Solar Power Photovoltaic (PV) System

A hardware-based academic project exploring the fundamentals and practical aspects of solar photovoltaic power generation — covering PV modules, charge controller, battery storage, and inverter stages.

**Status:** 🚧 Ongoing

---

## 📋 Overview

This project studies how solar energy is converted into usable electrical energy through a complete PV power generation system. It covers the full energy flow — from sunlight hitting the PV panel to usable AC power at the output — and evaluates the efficiency and reliability of each stage.

---

## ⚙️ System Components

- **PV Panels** – Convert solar radiation into DC electrical energy
- **Charge Controller** – Regulates voltage/current from the PV panel to prevent battery overcharge or discharge
- **Battery Energy Storage** – Stores generated power for use when sunlight is unavailable
- **Inverter** – Converts stored DC power into usable AC power for practical loads

---

## 🔬 What's Being Studied

- Conversion efficiency of solar energy into electrical energy
- Factors affecting PV output (irradiance, temperature, load conditions)
- Role of the charge controller in maintaining stable power delivery
- Inverter operation for DC-to-AC conversion
- System performance under varying operating conditions
