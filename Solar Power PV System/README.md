Solar Power PV System

Project Overview

The Solar Power Photovoltaic (PV) System is a renewable energy project that demonstrates the conversion of sunlight into electrical energy using the photovoltaic effect. The system uses solar PV panels to generate DC electricity, which can be converted into AC electricity using an inverter for practical applications.

Problem Statement

The increasing demand for electricity and the depletion of conventional energy resources have created a need for clean and sustainable energy solutions. Solar energy provides a renewable alternative, particularly for off-grid and rural applications. However, challenges such as initial installation cost, efficiency, energy storage, and variation in solar radiation need to be considered.

Objectives

To understand the conversion of solar energy into electrical energy using the photovoltaic effect.
To study the major components of a Solar PV system.
To provide a clean and renewable source of electrical energy.
To support power supply in remote and rural areas.
To understand the role of battery storage for backup power.

Working Principle

The Solar PV panel converts sunlight into DC electrical energy through the photovoltaic effect. The generated DC power is regulated by the charge controller. When a battery is included, excess electrical energy can be stored for later use. The inverter converts DC power into AC power for supplying electrical loads.

System Flow

Solar PV Panel → Charge Controller → Battery → Inverter → Electrical Load

Methodology

The system methodology involves selecting a suitable location with adequate sunlight and minimal shading, assessing solar radiation, determining the load requirements, and selecting appropriate system components. The solar panels are installed at a suitable tilt angle and connected with the other components. System parameters such as voltage, current, and power output can be monitored to evaluate performance.
