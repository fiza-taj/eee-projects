Solar Power PV System

Project Overview

A hardware-based project focused on the generation of electrical power using photovoltaic (PV) technology. The project explores the fundamental principles of solar energy conversion and the operation of a solar power PV system.

Objectives

- To understand the working principle of a solar power PV system.
- To study the conversion of solar energy into electrical power.
- To understand the major components and their functions in a PV system.
- To gain practical knowledge of renewable energy-based power generation.

Major Components

- Solar PV Panel
- Charge Controller
- Battery
- Inverter
- Electrical Load

Technical Area

Renewable Energy | Solar Power | Photovoltaic Systems | Electrical Power Generation

Project Type

Hardware-Based Academic Project
